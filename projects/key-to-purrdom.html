Key to Purrdom

I designed most of the puzzles and levels for Key to Purrdom, a cat-themed 2D puzzle platformer. I also implemented gameplay and integrated audio in Unity.

Date
April 2025
Project type
Cross-degree team project
My role
Level designer and Unity gameplay developer
Methods used
Puzzle design, Unity, audio integration, playtesting

Game trailer

Teaching the mouse-to-platform connection

Colour-coded mice toggle matching platforms. Early rooms let players see which area changes when they interact with a mouse. Later puzzles ask them to choose which platforms they want active and in what order to toggle them.

Reading hesitation in a playtest

In classroom sessions I watched for players who could see the destination but couldn’t tell what opened the route. That helped me separate the challenge of finding a sequence from confusion about what a mouse controlled.

Final gameplay

I built the puzzles and interactive levels and integrated the audio. The art students on the team created the visual assets.
