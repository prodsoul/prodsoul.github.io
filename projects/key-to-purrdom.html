Key to Purrdom

Key to Purrdom is a 2D puzzle platformer about a cat navigating a dungeon. Colour-coded mice control platforms that open routes to keys. I designed most of the levels and mechanics and implemented gameplay and audio in Unity.

Date
April 2025
Project type
Cross-degree team project
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Level designer and Unity gameplay developer
Methods used
Puzzle design, Unity, audio integration, playtesting

Teaching the switch mechanic

The mice and platforms share a colour so players can connect a switch to the part of the room it changes. Early puzzles introduce that relationship in a small space. Later puzzles ask players to think about the order they change platforms in as they work towards a key.

What I watched for in playtests

I built the puzzles and gameplay interactions in Unity and integrated the audio. In classroom playtests with UX and game design students and art students, I watched for places where players could see a key but struggled to work out which action would open the route.

The playable levels

The team’s art students created the visual assets. My focus was the playable level layouts and how the mice, platforms and keys fit together. The gameplay video shows those interactions in sequence.
