Molten Motion

I built three Mega Man Maker levels to explore how the same movement rules can support Easy, Normal and Hard routes.

Date
Month · Year
Project type
Level design study
My role
Level designer
Methods used
Mega Man Maker, level design, difficulty progression

Teaching the obstacle with space to recover

I used lava, conveyors and the same industrial tiles across the levels. Easy introduces jumps with room to prepare and land. Normal asks the player to link actions across consecutive obstacles, giving them less time to reset between moves.

Planning the next movement on Hard

Hard combines narrower landings, spikes and moving surfaces. I used the next visible platform to show where the player should aim, then reduced the space available to stop before the next obstacle.

Reviewing how difficulty changes

I recorded a complete run of each level to compare their pacing. I looked at the amount of landing space, how often a player could recover and how far ahead they needed to plan.
