Molten Motion

Molten Motion is a three-level difficulty progression I built in Mega Man Maker. Easy, Normal and Hard use the same movement toolkit, with tighter timing and more hazards as the player advances.

Date
Month · Year
Project type
Level design study
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Level designer
Methods used
Mega Man Maker, level layout, difficulty progression

Introducing the route

I used the same industrial tiles, conveyors and lava hazards across all three levels so players could carry what they learned from one level to the next. The Easy route introduces gaps with room to set up a jump, and Normal starts asking players to time movement between consecutive obstacles.

Making the landings harder

For Hard, I combined narrow landings, spikes and moving surfaces. Shorter safe areas made the order and timing of jumps matter more. I used the placement of the next platform to signal where the player should aim, even when there was little space to stop.

Reviewing the difficulty curve

I recorded a full run of each level to review how the obstacle sequence changes. The runs show a progression from standalone jumps with safe landing room to combinations that ask players to plan the next move while they are still in motion.
