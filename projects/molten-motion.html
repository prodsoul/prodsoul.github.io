Molten Motion

  • Month · Year
  • Game & Level Design
  • Mega Man Maker, level design, difficulty progression
  • Level design study · Level designer

I built three Mega Man Maker levels to explore how the same movement rules can produce three different difficulties. I used a shared industrial setting and compared what Easy, Normal, and Hard ask the player to do with the space between hazards.

Easy — give players room to learn the jump

I introduced lava and moving surfaces with room to approach and recover. A landing with space to stop lets a player see what happens after the jump and read the next obstacle at their own pace.

Normal — bring obstacles closer together

I reused familiar elements, including conveyors, but shortened the space between them. A player can still recognise each obstacle on sight. The challenge is to finish one action in a position that sets up the next one.

Hard — ask the player to plan the landing

I combined narrower landings, spikes, and moving surfaces. The next platform stays visible so the player has a target, but there’s less room to stop or correct the approach once they land.

Compare the three routes in motion

I recorded a full run of each level and compared how often I could stop, recover, and look ahead. If I tested them with other players, I’d watch for the first place each person hesitates or retries and check whether it’s the timing, the landing space, or the sequence that makes the difference.
