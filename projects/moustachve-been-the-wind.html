Moustach’ve Been the Wind

Moustach’ve Been the Wind is a 2D speedrunning platformer about an octopus barber racing a clock. I led our eight-person team through six milestones and worked on movement, level design and the handoff from Unity to our custom engine.

Date
September 2025 – April 2026
Project type
Team project
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Project manager and game designer
Methods used
Unity prototyping, level design, playtesting, JSON export

Designing for momentum

Mr Mouch builds momentum as he runs. Skid turns, wall kicks and a ground pound that carries into a roll let players keep that momentum through a room. Running also slows the clock’s drain, giving players a reason to practise a route and chain moves together.

From Unity to the custom engine

I prototyped and playtested the movement in Unity alongside a programmer and documented the controls and level rules. When the programming team moved the game to a custom C++ engine, I built a JSON export workflow to carry over the tested tile layouts, layers and collision. That let the team retain the routes we had already playtested.

The final game

Our team completed the playable game over six milestones. I coordinated level and audio decisions. The programming team built the custom engine, and our artist created the trailer. The gameplay shows how the final routes give players room to build momentum and find quicker lines.
