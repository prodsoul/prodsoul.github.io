Order Up!

Order Up! is a real-time card game for three to five players racing to complete four matching sets of dish cards. I made paper prototypes, drafted the first rulebook and prepared the final card layouts for print.

Date
March 2025
Project type
Team project
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Game design, prototyping and print production
Methods used
Paper prototyping, playtesting, rules writing, Illustrator

A real-time race for dishes

Everyone plays at the same time, exchanging dish cards with a central pantry. Finishing a set gives a player the option to use a chef ability, which can help their own hand or interfere with someone else’s. The round ends when a player finishes four sets.

Testing at the intended pace

Our team tried several paper concepts before settling on the card game. Early sessions that taught the game one turn at a time missed the pressure of simultaneous play. In later sessions we tested specific details, including when players could use abilities and when everyone needed to pause so an effect could resolve.

Taking the game to print

I drafted the first rules and assembled the final card layouts in Illustrator using artwork from my teammates. Going from paper to a printed game made it clear that the rulebook, card layout and timing of play all affect how easily a group can learn it.
