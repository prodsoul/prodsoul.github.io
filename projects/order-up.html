Order Up!

Order Up! is a real-time card game for three to five players. I made paper prototypes, drafted the first rulebook and prepared the final card layouts for print.

Date
March 2025
Project type
Team project
My role
Game design, prototyping and print production
Methods used
Paper playtesting, rules writing, Illustrator

The pressure of a shared pantry

Everyone exchanges dish cards with the shared pantry at the same time. Completing a set lets a player choose whether to use a chef ability, and completing four sets ends the round.

Testing at the pace we designed for

Our group tried several paper concepts before settling on this game. Early sessions that taught the actions one turn at a time missed the pressure of everyone playing simultaneously.

Later playtests focused on when players could use abilities and when the table needed to pause for an effect to resolve. That gave us a more precise way to investigate where the rules interrupted play.

From paper prototype to print

I drafted the first rules and assembled the final Illustrator layouts with my teammates’ art. In print, the card layouts and the rulebook had to work together so a group could set up and start playing from the components on the table.
