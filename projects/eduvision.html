EduVision

EduVision is an Android field guide for young learners exploring insects in Singapore. I manage milestones and implement the interface from Figma in Unity.

Date
September 2026 – Present
Project type
Team project
My role
Project manager and UX designer
Methods used
UX research, Figma, Unity UI, Jira

From noticing an insect to learning about it

We designed around a real situation outdoors. A learner notices an insect, photographs it and wants to know what it is. The identification result gives them a way into the 3D specimen and the information that goes with it.

Giving learners a choice of what to explore

I worked on the transitions between capture, identification and the viewer. The result supports a quick read. Body-part labels and audio support a closer look, and a field journal gives learners a way to revisit a discovery after they leave the park.

My work across design and development

I track milestones and priorities in Jira and build the screens in Unity. I work with the teammates responsible for recognition, content authoring, and 3D and AR systems. Their outputs determine what the interface can show while a learner is waiting and what information is available once a specimen opens.

The current prototype

The current build gives us a way to review the capture-to-exploration journey. The next questions are where young learners pause, whether they understand the result, and whether they can return to something they saved.
