EduVision

EduVision is an Android field guide for young learners exploring insects in Singapore. I manage milestones in Jira and design and implement the interface from Figma into Unity.

Date
September 2026 – Present
Project type
Team project
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Project manager and UX designer
Methods used
UX research, Figma, Unity UI, Jira

From a sighting to a discovery

The experience starts outdoors with an insect a learner has noticed. They take a photo, check the identification result and open an interactive specimen. Body-part labels and audio descriptions give them a way to explore details at their own pace. Their field journal keeps the discovery available for later.

Designing the learning flow

I designed the screens as a sequence and implemented the interface in Unity. The result gives a quick overview, the 3D view supports closer inspection, and the journal keeps past finds easy to revisit. That structure helps a learner decide whether to read, explore or get back to looking for insects.

Current prototype

I track sprint priorities and milestones while working with teammates responsible for species recognition, content authoring and the 3D and AR systems. The project is ongoing. The screens show the current prototype and how those parts fit into one experience.
