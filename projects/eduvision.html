EduVision

September 2026 – Present Learning & Design Tools UX research, Figma, Unity UI, Jira Team project · Project manager and UX designer

EduVision is a field guide for young learners exploring insects in Singapore. I manage milestones and turn our Figma flows into Unity screens, working with teammates who build the recognition, content, and 3D and AR systems.

Start with an insect someone just found

We designed around a moment that can happen in a park. A learner spots an insect, takes a photo, and wants to know what it is. I worked on how capture leads to an identification result and how that result gives them a way into the specimen without breaking the thread of what they just found.

Let learners choose how far to explore

The identification result supports a quick read. The viewer is for taking a closer look. Learners can rotate the specimen, open body-part information, and listen to descriptions. The field journal gives them a place to revisit discoveries later, when they may have more time to explore.

Carry the screen flow from Figma into Unity

I work with engineering teammates to connect the screens to the systems behind them. For example, recognition determines what the interface can say while someone is waiting and what details are ready when the result opens. I track work across milestones in Jira so the interface and those systems can be reviewed together.

What I’d check with learners next

The prototype gives us a way to try the journey from capture to exploration. I’d ask a learner to photograph an insect, tell me what they think the result means, then find something specific on the model. I’d also check whether they can return to the same discovery through the journal without help.
