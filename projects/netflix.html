Netflix Mobile Discovery

I researched how viewers choose what to watch and designed a mobile Explore flow that lets people combine filters and compare ratings from the results.

Date
November 2025
Project type
Solo concept redesign
My role
UX researcher and designer
Methods used
Survey, competitive analysis, journey mapping, Figma

Where choosing broke down

I surveyed ten viewers and compared four other streaming apps. In the survey, ease of choosing what to watch averaged 2.4 out of 5. I used two personas and their browsing journeys to identify where a viewer with a rough idea would still have trouble narrowing the options.

Building a shortlist in Explore

I prototyped the browsing route on paper and took it into Figma. Expandable filter groups let viewers apply several criteria together. The results show ratings beside the posters, so viewers can compare shortlisted titles before opening individual detail pages.

What prototype feedback showed

Feedback highlighted the value of combining criteria and seeing relevant details before opening a title. A follow-up test could compare how readily viewers narrow the catalogue and choose a title in the original and redesigned flows.
