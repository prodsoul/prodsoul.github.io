Netflix Mobile Discovery

I researched how people choose what to watch and redesigned Netflix’s mobile discovery flow. The Figma prototype puts ratings in view and lets viewers combine filters on an Explore page.

Date
November 2025
Project type
Solo concept redesign
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
UX researcher and designer
Methods used
User surveys, competitive analysis, journey mapping, Figma

Where viewers got stuck

I surveyed ten viewers and compared four other streaming apps. Survey participants rated the ease of choosing what to watch at an average of 2.4 out of 5. I used two personas and their browsing journeys to identify where someone with a rough idea of what they wanted would still get stuck.

Designing the Explore flow

I took the Explore flow from paper to Figma. The filter menu uses expandable groups so viewers can narrow titles by more than one criterion at a time. Filtered results surface ratings alongside the posters, giving viewers a way to rule titles in or out before opening each detail page.

What prototype feedback showed

Prototype feedback highlighted the value of combining filters and seeing relevant details in the results. The final concept shows how viewers could reduce a large catalogue to a shortlist they can compare.
