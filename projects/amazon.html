Amazon Search & Prime

In a four-person concept project, I redesigned Amazon’s mobile filter and Prime membership flows. I focused on making search choices visible and membership plans easier to compare.

Date
October 2025
Project type
Team concept redesign
My role
Search filters and membership designer
Methods used
Heuristic evaluation, paper prototyping, Figma

Two points of friction

Our review found it was hard to tell which search filters were active and difficult to compare Prime options across repeated prompts. I took both flows from paper sketches through low-fidelity wireframes and into high-fidelity screens.

Keeping filter choices visible

I reduced the quick-filter bar to four entry points and put the full list of options in a bottom sheet. The design puts selected values at the top, marks active groups, and lets shoppers reset one group at a time without losing the others.

Comparing Prime plans

I put monthly and annual plans side by side so shoppers could review prices and benefits together before signing up. My teammates designed the other shopping and account flows.
