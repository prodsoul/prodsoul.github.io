Amazon Mobile Redesign

I redesigned Amazon’s mobile filters and Prime membership screens in a four-person concept project. My aim was to make it easier to narrow a search, see which filters were active and compare membership options.

Date
October 2025
Project type
Team concept redesign
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Search filters and membership designer
Methods used
Heuristic evaluation, paper prototyping, Figma

Two points of friction

Our review surfaced two problems in my part of the app. Search made it hard to tell which filters had been applied, and Prime information was scattered across repeated prompts. I took both flows through paper sketches, low-fidelity wireframes and high-fidelity screens.

Making search filters visible

For search, I pared the quick-filter bar back to four entry points and moved the full set of controls into a bottom sheet. Selected options move to the top, active groups are marked, and shoppers can reset one group at a time. I kept common filters in predictable positions and gave product-specific options their own section.

Comparing Prime plans

For membership, I put the monthly and annual plans side by side so shoppers could compare pricing and benefits before signing up. The case study shows my filter and membership work. My teammates handled the other shopping and account flows.
