GIO Guest Booking Experience

I created the identity, website and mobile app concept for GIO, a Taoist-inspired bar. The guest experience connects discovering the venue, browsing its menu and reserving a table.

Date
May 2025 – June 2025
Project type
Solo project
My role
Brand and UX designer
Methods used
Visual identity, Figma, A/B testing

Connecting the venue and the interface

I used the bar’s Yin and Yang theme to connect the brand, website and mobile concept. The site introduces the venue, and the app gives returning guests quick access to its menu, reservations and profile.

Testing animation and menu choices

I ran four A/B comparisons covering the speed of the opening talisman scroll, when sign-in buttons appear, how drink cards are laid out and how guests enter a themed zone. In the speed comparison, 72% preferred the slower opening, which I kept.

I also used the menu layout that puts the drink name and price below the photo. It gives the image space and keeps the information guests need together.

From browsing to booking

The Figma concept connects the atmospheric introduction to practical steps. A guest can review drinks, start a booking and see confirmation at the end of the reservation flow.
