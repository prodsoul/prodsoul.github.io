GIO Guest Booking Experience

I created the identity, website and mobile app concept for GIO, a Taoist-inspired bar. The guest experience takes people from discovering the bar and browsing its drinks to reserving a table.

Date
May 2025 – June 2025
Project type
Solo project
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Brand and UX designer
Methods used
Visual identity, Figma, A/B testing

From discovering the bar to booking

I carried the bar’s Yin and Yang theme through the brand and the guest-facing prototype. The website introduces the venue, while the mobile concept gives returning guests quick routes to the drinks menu, reservations and their profile.

Testing the pacing and menu

I ran four A/B comparisons covering the pace of the opening talisman scroll, when sign-in buttons appear, how drink cards are arranged and how guests enter a themed zone. In the animation comparison, 72% preferred the slower version. I kept that timing and used the drink card layout that places the name and price below the photo.

The guest experience

The Figma prototype let me test the bar’s atmosphere and the practical booking tasks in the same flow. The opening has room for the brand’s pacing, while the menu and reservation screens make the information guests need easy to scan.
