GIO Guest Booking Experience

  • May 2025 – June 2025
  • Digital Product UX
  • Visual identity, Figma, A/B testing
  • Solo project · Brand and UX designer

I designed the identity, website, and mobile app concept for GIO, a Taoist-inspired bar. I wanted someone discovering the venue to get a sense of its atmosphere and still be able to find the drinks and reserve a table.

View Mobile Prototype HereView Web Prototype Here

Give the website and app different jobs

The website introduces the venue and its Yin and Yang areas. The app brings the menu, reservations, and profile into a flow a guest can return to. I used the same visual identity across them so the connection stays clear as guests move from looking around to booking.

Test when to ask guests to act

I compared two speeds for the opening talisman scroll. In my A/B study, 72% of respondents preferred the slower version, so I kept it. I also compared showing the sign-in buttons during the animation with showing them once it finished. The second version gave guests a clearer point to make their choice.

Make the menu easy to read

One drink-card layout puts the name above the photo. The other places the name and price together below it. I kept the second layout after testing because it puts the information guests compare in one place while leaving room for the image. I also used a swipe to enter a themed zone, based on feedback that it suited the experience.

Follow the journey through to booking

I connected the menu and reservation screens through to a booking confirmation in Figma. If I tested the flows again, I’d ask first-time visitors to pick a drink and book a table from both the site and the app, then check where they lose sight of the next step.
