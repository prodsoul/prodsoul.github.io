Order Kopi Lah!

October 2026 – November 2026 Playtesting & Analysis Survey design, mobile playtesting, iteration Team project · Usability researcher

Order Kopi Lah! is a mobile sorting puzzle set in a Singapore kopitiam. I led two rounds of playtesting to see how people read orders, recognised which boosters they could use, and found new vocabulary in the Lingo Book.

Play Game Here

Test the decisions players make in a level

Players sort items to fulfil orders written in kopitiam lingo, such as Kopi C or Teh Tarik. I wrote a 13-question survey and ran two rounds with three testers each. I asked about decisions that could interrupt play: working out what a clear would reveal, identifying available boosters, and finding what they had just unlocked.

Make booster and reward states easier to read

In the first round, available and empty boosters looked similar. My teammates faded the empty states and clarified their feedback. We also added a dot on the Lingo Book tab after a level clear so players had a cue to check the words they’d unlocked.

Check how the changes read in the next round

All three testers in the second round rated booster availability at least 4 out of 5, compared with none in the first round. All three also said they found new words without help, compared with one of three in the first round. Those are self-reported results from two small rounds.

What I’d test next

Players still had some difficulty predicting which items would appear after a clear. I’d ask a first-time player to predict the next layer before making a move, then see whether the board gives them enough information to explain their answer. My teammate built the playable web version linked here.
