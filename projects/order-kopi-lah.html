Order Kopi Lah!

Order Kopi Lah! is a mobile sorting puzzle set in a Singapore kopitiam. I tested how players read drink orders, understood boosters and discovered new vocabulary in the Lingo Book.

Date
October 2026 – November 2026
Project type
Team project
Organisation
DigiPen Institute of Technology
My role
Usability researcher
Methods used
Survey design, mobile playtesting, prototype comparison

What I tested

Players match ingredients to orders written in kopitiam lingo, such as Kopi C or Teh Tarik. I wrote a 13-question survey and ran two mobile playtest rounds with three players each. I documented points of confusion and compared responses across the two versions.

Changes between playtests

In the first round, one of three testers said they found new words without help. The team added a Lingo Book notification after a level clear, clarified booster states and adjusted shop prices.

In the second round, all three testers said they found new words unaided. All three rated booster availability at least 4 out of 5, up from zero in the first round.

What to test next

A useful next test would check whether first-time players can predict what is underneath a set before clearing it. Some participants had to watch a clear first. I ran the sessions, wrote the survey and documented the findings. My teammates built and revised the prototype.
