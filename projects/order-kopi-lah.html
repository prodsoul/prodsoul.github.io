Order Kopi Lah!

Order Kopi Lah! is a mobile sorting puzzle set in a Singapore kopitiam. I tested how players read orders, understood boosters and discovered vocabulary in the Lingo Book.

Date
October 2026 – November 2026
Project type
Team project
My role
Usability researcher
Methods used
Survey design, mobile playtesting, iteration

What I tested

Players match ingredients to drink orders that use kopitiam lingo, such as Kopi C or Teh Tarik. I wrote a 13-question survey and ran two playtest rounds with three players each. I looked at how people interpreted the board, identified available boosters and found words after a level.

Changes between rounds

In the first round, one of three testers reported finding new words without help. The team added a Lingo Book notification after a level clear, clarified booster states and adjusted shop prices.

What the second round showed

All three testers said they found new words unaided. All three rated booster availability at least 4 out of 5, up from zero in round one. Those results describe the six responses across both small rounds.

Some players still needed to see a set clear before they understood what was underneath it. I’d test whether a first-time player can predict that result. I owned the sessions, survey and findings. My teammates built and revised the prototype.
